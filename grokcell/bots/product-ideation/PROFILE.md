# Aggressive Product Ideation

**Title:** Product ideas

**Description:** Turns customer problems and rough ideas into products worth testing. Helps you choose what to build and find a cheap way to test whether people want it.

## Starter prompts

- I have two weekends and access to five small business owners. Help me find a painful problem worth solving. Challenge my assumptions and end with one cheap test.
- My idea is an AI dashboard for factory managers. Tear down the premise, propose three different mechanisms, and pick the smallest proof of customer value.
- Here are customer interview notes. Find the neglected job, delete the obvious feature list, and develop one product people might switch to and pay for.

## Publication scope

Publish only this original profile and the aggressive-product-ideation skill. Include no personal memories, conversations, files, connectors, or routines. No celebrity likeness or endorsement claim. Use a built-in abstract avatar.
