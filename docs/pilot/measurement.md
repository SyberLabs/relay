# Measurement specification

Protocol v1; source inspection at [`fe91bb3bd2faa087680eacae246d31d7b7eb27d1`](https://github.com/SyberLabs/relay/tree/fe91bb3bd2faa087680eacae246d31d7b7eb27d1), 2026-09-07. This is a read-only code inventory, not a live deployment or analytics audit. Recheck mappings against the participant's actual build; never assign this commit to Ravi by assumption. Manual records are sufficient for this pilot. Missing events below are specifications of evidence gaps, not implemented telemetry or prerequisites for running the study.

## Units, cohort, and clock

- **Participant:** anonymous ID, one person counted once. Five qualified consenting people enrolled before observing their first attempt. The [brief](README.md) supplies qualification criteria. No replacement because someone fails, needs help, stops, or does not return.
- **Task:** one independently meaningful real application or follow-up deliverable. Assign an opaque task ID in private records. A new job qualifies as different work; a new follow-up purpose on the same job can too. Reformats, stale-result retries, alternate prompts, rereview, or another draft of the same deliverable remain the same task. Job IDs alone cannot establish task distinctness.
- **Initial attempt:** from first opening Relay for the chosen work to core completion or participant stop. Allow about 45 minutes for scheduling; there is no speed threshold. If scheduling ends the attempt, record the incomplete stages and that constraint. Neutral baseline interviews precede it. Log onboarding, fresh fact verification, transfers, self-directed retries, and help. Developer preparation of personal task material means the attempt was assisted even if it occurred before the clock.
- **Return window:** let `t0 = session_end_utc`, after the entire initial session including optional rescue and debrief. Let `t1 = t0 + 336 hours` (14 × 24 hours). Count a qualifying substantive return in **(t0, t1]**. UTC avoids daylight-saving ambiguity. Store timestamps with `Z`; the reporting date never substitutes for the occurrence date.
- **As-of:** `observed_through_utc` is the latest time for which coverage is established, not merely today's date. A retrospective report can establish coverage through `t1`; label it reported. A known return does not prove continuous visibility over the rest of the window.

Do not reset or lengthen a window for a new build, outage, reminder, assistance, or retry. Record outages and exposure changes as confounders. Build changes during a cohort remain visible by participant/session; do not claim a single-version result. Unknown or approximate `t0` means uncertain cutoff; preserve the timestamp range privately and classify borderline activity unknown.

**Incomplete observations:** mark `open` before cutoff, `mature` only when cutoff has passed and behavior through it is established, and `unknown` when coverage cannot be established (including loss to follow-up). `withdrawn` records withdrawn consent with only the information permitted to be retained. No-event observations alone do not establish a non-return. For each threshold show `confirmed successes / planned 5`, known non-successes, unresolved cases, and qualified count. For return, the possible final count lies between confirmed qualifying returners and that number plus unresolved participants who could still qualify. An early confirmed return can enter the lower bound while its window is open; do not declare final cohort success/failure from that alone.

If fewer than five people are confirmed qualified, report the evidence with its actual eligible count and the planned denominator. Do not silently lower the rule to 3/n or remove unsuccessful qualified people. If eligibility is disproved later, retain that record as exploratory, disclose why the planned cohort is incomplete, and predeclare any further enrollment in #5 before observing it. Unknown eligibility is unresolved, not a favorable exclusion. A participant who qualifies initially but gets no new task stays in the cohort; report lack of opportunity as one possible explanation for non-return. With five people and convenience recruitment, describe behavior without population or causal claims.

## Metrics and their evidence

### M1. Independent core completion — threshold at least 3 of 5

One success requires all of these during the initial attempt, on participant-chosen real work: own job/research brought into Relay; candidate facts deliberately selected (record saved reuse versus fresh entry); actual transfer to an external assistant; result returned to the same job/version; human review and explicit exact acceptance persisted and recovered; accepted wording retrieved for an application/follow-up preparation destination. No submission is required. Count once per person regardless of retries. Developer or other helper task guidance, transcription, personalized setup, account repair, or completing a step makes completion **assisted**, not independent. Self-directed use of normal docs/assistant is allowed and timed. Safe rejection of a bad draft is good judgment but incomplete for this metric; record why.

Existing evidence: [`app/api/workspace/route.ts`](../../app/api/workspace/route.ts) `save` writes `Draft accepted` when requested status is `Ready`, otherwise `Review saved`. Details include `status`, `draft`, `blocker`; job state contains `draft`, `accepted_draft`, and incremented `version`. [`lib/editor.ts`](../../lib/editor.ts) `showsExactAcceptance` checks matching text, version, clean editor, and Ready status. [`lib/workspace-events.ts`](../../lib/workspace-events.ts) retrieves historical accepted text. These can corroborate a persisted acceptance, not reading, real task identity, independent completion, or actual external transfer. A repeated save while Ready may emit another acceptance; event totals are not people or unique successful handoffs.

The same owner-scoped route's `progress` action delegates to [`lib/application-progress.ts`](../../lib/application-progress.ts), which records `Progress saved` with the note, blocker, input version, and operation ID. It updates the blocker, version, and update time while preserving `draft`, `accepted_draft`, and application status. A matching operation replay does not create another event. This corroborates saved activity, not fresh acceptance, assistant transfer, distinct task purpose, or independent completion.

Manual evidence: stage log, no-intervention determination, task reality, assistant actually receiving context, private comparison of persisted and retrieved text, and destination reuse. **Missing events:** job-entry completion classification, handoff actually shared/returned, review viewed, accepted-text retrieved or reused. Do not invent these from page visits, successful download, or a `Review saved` / `Progress saved` event.

**Current follow-up limit:** [`lib/domain.ts`](../../lib/domain.ts) `validateEdit` preserves Submitted/Live loop/terminal statuses; [`app/workspace.tsx`](../../app/workspace.tsx) exposes “Save notes and draft” for these jobs. Saving notes and draft then emits `Review saved`, clears `accepted_draft`, and does not create fresh exact acceptance. “Save progress only” instead records `Progress saved` while retaining the existing draft and accepted wording; it also cannot accept new wording. A participant choosing that real task remains valid; record the blocked acceptance stage. Do not force a new application, reset status to Ready, or credit an old accepted event for new wording. This is an observed code capability limit, not an implemented fix in the next-task criteria.

### M2. Voluntary different real handoff — threshold at least 2 of the same 5

Count a person once when, after `t0` and by `t1`, they independently choose a different real task and actually share a Relay-prepared handoff with their external assistant. They must use Relay substantively for that task (select/retrieve its context or facts), not merely open it. Record whether they then complete review, acceptance, and reuse separately; return does not imply successful completion. Initial non-completers may qualify as returners. This operational definition tests returning **for** a handoff without requiring a later employer submission.

Manual evidence: later-work record with occurrence time, distinct purpose, what prompted it, actual transfer evidence or a concrete participant report, and any prior contacts. Ask the return reason openly before naming context/wording. Classify preserved research, saved candidate facts, exact accepted wording, unrelated feature, novelty/social obligation, or unknown based on what they describe. A timestamp or second job alone cannot show intent.

Existing evidence: owner-scoped `Review saved`, `Draft accepted`, and `Progress saved` events may corroborate later activity, but none proves transfer, distinct purpose, or voluntary choice. **Missing events:** session/visit start, task-purpose identifier, transfer, and return trigger. Even future transfer events cannot establish voluntariness without observation/report.

Exclude examples, developer QA, instructed exercise repeats, same-task retries, visit-only activity, and task work caused by reminders or incentives. Log all reminders. Conservatively exclude subsequent activity after an in-window use request/return incentive from the primary voluntary count unless the substantive return is documented as already having occurred before that prompt. Ordinary support requested after an independent substantive return does not undo that return; assistance still affects subsequent completion. Unknown trigger or ordering remains unknown. Do not send research follow-ups during the window just to measure return.

### M3. Actual effort and concrete benefit

For every attempt, record active seconds by stage, non-overlapping system/assistant wait, and unrelated interruption time. `relay_active_min` includes setup, choosing/verifying facts, retyping, file/clipboard work, corrections, retries, and review through core completion or stop. Include shared troubleshooting attention in participant active time; report developer time separately, without adding it twice. Initial baseline/debrief interview time is research overhead, kept separately in the private record. Task elapsed time = active + wait + interruptions; reconcile to start/end and explain gaps. Do not call wall time active effort.

For a comparable completed baseline and Relay task, **net active minutes saved = baseline_active_min − relay_active_min**. Positive means less effort with Relay; negative means added effort. Only calculate when both active times and completed endpoints are known and task/output comparability is defensible. Retain `observed` versus `recalled` baseline method. A recalled comparison yields estimated savings, never measured savings. Leave savings blank for an incomplete Relay task, missing times, or incomparable work; report its wasted effort anyway. Record first-use setup cost separately in stage notes rather than hiding it through assumed amortization. Time each later real task anew.

Benefit requires a concrete account of what was reused and which baseline step was avoided, alongside added work and output usefulness (`usable_unchanged`, `usable_after_edits`, `unusable`, `unknown`). Count retyped versus reused facts, reconstruction episodes, and corrections in stage notes where observable. Report paired participant values and how many pairs are comparable; a single five-person average can hide failure. Quality or preserved certainty can be valuable even with negative minutes saved, but name the tradeoff instead of asserting time savings.

Existing sources contain no reliable active-time, intervention, retry, usefulness, or baseline data. `jobs.effort` is a planning input/default, not measured task duration. Event timestamp differences and model response durations cannot substitute for active time. **Missing events:** stage timing, retries/friction, source/fact/accepted-wording reuse. Manual observations remain necessary for baseline and usefulness regardless of telemetry.

### M4. Actual reuse and downstream outcomes (descriptive)

Distinguish persisted acceptance, retrieval, movement to an unsent destination, changed wording, and reported submission. [`app/api/outcomes/route.ts`](../../app/api/outcomes/route.ts) records `Outcome: submitted` and other `Outcome: <kind>` events plus `outcomes` rows with `occurred` and `created`. `occurred` is user supplied or defaulted; `created` is record time. [`lib/outcomes.ts`](../../lib/outcomes.ts) requires Ready status and a receipt string for local submission and deduplicates receipted submitted job IDs for response-rate evidence. Imported submissions without receipts are excluded from those rates.

These are **participant-reported outcomes** with a required receipt reference, not independent proof of transmission or the exact submitted wording. The code validates receipt shape, not the external receipt's truth. Record private confirmation if voluntarily supplied; do not inspect email or contact an employer. A saved draft, staged draft, or hiring outcome is not retention or evidence that Relay caused better hiring results. **Missing link:** accepted text/version to exact wording used externally. Manual comparison is needed; no runtime changes are made here.

### M5. Later payment evidence

The [unsent interview/offer](README.md#later-willingness-to-pay-interview--offer-draft) records actual baseline spending, response to the US$10/30-day offer, checkout request, paid amount/date, refunds, and later paid use separately. Source: private interview and independently recorded payment if an authorized offer is later made. **Missing:** billing/payment events; no payment flow is implemented or assumed. Hypothetical willingness has no payment count.

## Other existing records and extraction limits

- [`db/schema.ts`](../../db/schema.ts) `events` stores `id`, `owner`, `job_id`, `kind`, `detail`, `created`. It has no study participant ID, task-purpose ID, build, handoff ID, or explicit accepted version field. Keep study links in private records, not schema changes.
- Workspace `GET` returns only the latest **200** events across the owner. The per-job `history` action defaults to **50**, maximum **100**, with a strict `created < before` cursor. Paginate relevant history with authorized access, deduplicate by event ID, and note that equal timestamps at page boundaries can leave gaps. A partial feed cannot prove non-use. Do not export raw `detail`, owner IDs, or drafts to Git.
- Workspace import/bootstrap adds jobs and observations, but no study import event. Ordinary Add job is another path through that import contract: [`lib/first-job.ts`](../../lib/first-job.ts) builds a Held source row with `first-job:<key>` as its source URL, stored as observation `source_url`; this is not a study event or evidence of participant arrival. Preview/replay returns a classification without persistence. Source observation dates may be inherited and job `updated` may reflect import/save, not participant arrival. Seed records, source counts, and imports cannot identify real participants or handoffs.
- [`app/connections.tsx`](../../app/connections.tsx) keeps `facts`, `nextTask`, and selected `research` in client state; copy/download/paste/load provide local feedback without durable events. [`lib/integration-files.ts`](../../lib/integration-files.ts) binds current packets to job identity/key/version; ChatGPT/Codex/Obsidian results require matching IDs. [`lib/assistant-handoff.ts`](../../lib/assistant-handoff.ts) emits a review-required result and separates unverified research from supplied facts. Current packets do not bind selected saved fact IDs or a profile version.
- [`app/api/profile/route.ts`](../../app/api/profile/route.ts) and [`lib/store.ts`](../../lib/store.ts) store profile facts and increment profile version. The workspace loads usable facts for fit, but Connections does not select them from the ledger. [`app/api/drafts/route.ts`](../../app/api/drafts/route.ts) separately logs cited drafts with profile version, review batches/corrections, refusal signatures, and sometimes `Draft staged by agent`. Logging, correction, and staging are not exact workspace acceptance and are not assured evidence for the manual Connections path.

The [earlier assisted trial comment](https://github.com/SyberLabs/relay/issues/5#issuecomment-5556370826) reports 80.9 seconds elapsed across orchestration/generation/observation/transcription, with active user time unmeasured. It reports three records, not three participants; drafts remained unaccepted. Preserve that as QA evidence only. Do not use its timing as a baseline or its activity as cohort completion/return.

Before publishing a decision, reconcile each CSV summary to its private stage/visit record, check membership and clock arithmetic, and distinguish observed, reported, event-corroborated, and unknown evidence. Never publish raw research to make the counts look more credible.
