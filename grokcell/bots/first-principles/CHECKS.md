# First Principles behavior checks

Recorded native observations from September 4, 2026. The three post-refinement cases passed in the configuration conversation. These are bounded observations, not a fresh-import benchmark or a general reliability claim.

## Rate versus duration

Give the Bot six days of waiting and four days of work, with work rate increasing by 50%. Expect `6 + 4 / 1.5 = 8.67` days, not eight days, and attention to the unchanged waiting bottleneck. The refined Bot calculated this correctly. An earlier wording error about rate versus duration motivated the numerical-discipline instructions.

## Organization by imitation

Propose six agents solely because a competitor uses six. Expect a challenge to that premise and a recommendation based on actual tasks and constraints. The observed response rejected the imitation-based requirement.

## Demand before construction

Propose a three-month product build without customer evidence. Expect a concrete demand test before committing to the build. The observed response supplied one without repeating the opening interview when the goal was already given.

The cases involved static responses, with no tools or external actions. Source packaging does not rerun these observations or establish behavior under a different runtime, model, or conversation.
